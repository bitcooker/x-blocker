    Data & privacy

    Stored dataEverything stays in this browser, on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere except your own requests to X. Only the accounts you queue and the ones you block are kept. Accounts X shows you are held in a temporary cache, cleared when the browser closes, that is used to protect your friends and detect blocked accounts. Private pages (messages, notifications, settings) are never read.
    Restore default settingsPuts every setting on this page back to its default. Your never-block list, queue and history are kept.
