Import a list

Paste @handles, profile links or account IDs (id:12345), one per line or separated by commas. You can also choose a .txt or .csv file, including a Blocked or Queue export from X Blocker. Everything you add is queued as one batch.

Batches

Each list you block or import is a batch. Cancel the accounts still waiting, or undo a batch to unblock everyone in it.

    Data & privacy

    Stored dataEverything stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere except your own requests to X. X Blocker saves only the accounts you queue and block. Other accounts you see are kept in memory until the browser closes, to protect people you follow. Messages, notifications and settings pages are never read.
